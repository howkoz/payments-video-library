The new dunning program, to date

How we retry a declined subscription rebill in the United States and Canada, and how it is doing. For Customer Service, Finance and anyone outside Payments. Records to 27 November 2026.

Part 1. What changed

Which declines are retriedEvery declined rebillOnly wording in the RETRY group
WhenOnce, 12 hours after the declineDay 4, then day 8 if the bank says "not now" again

WhereUnited States and CanadaUnited States and Canada

The settings sit in the production configuration: 2 retries per order, waits of 3 days and 4 days.

Part 2. How the bank's wording is sorted

The bank sends a reason with every decline. The program matches that wording against its rules and puts it in one group.

GroupWhat the bank meansWhat we do
RETRYNot now: not enough funds, limit exceeded, withdrawal limitRetry on day 4
UPDATEThe card expired or closed. The customer needs a new card.No retry
STOPNever: lost, stolen, fraud, stop all recurring paymentsNo retry
FIXOur own setup was wrong. A retry would fail the same way.No retry
UNKNOWNWording the rules do not knowNo retry
The cautious rule. When a wording matches two groups, the most cautious one wins. A wording that matches both STOP and RETRY is never retried.

Day by day

  1. Day 0: the rebill is declined. The program sorts the wording.
  2. Only a RETRY wording in the United States or Canada joins the retry list.
  3. Day 4: retry 1 runs, 72 hours after the decline.
  4. If it pays, the order is finished.
  5. If retry 1 is declined with a RETRY wording, or one the rules do not know, the order waits for day 8.
  6. STOP, UPDATE, FIX or "Do Not Honor" ends the retries.
  7. Day 8: retry 2 runs, 96 hours after retry 1. This is the last attempt.
  8. Nothing is charged after day 14.

Part 3. How it is doing

We compared the same days of two months. Each order counts once, by its first retry. The orders were first declined on day 4 to day 23 of each month. October ran the old model. November ran the new one.

  • Both markets: 258 of 3,161 first retries paid (8.2%) in October. 428 of 3,639 paid (11.8%) in November.
  • United States: 223 of 2,641 (8.4%) rose to 329 of 3,094 (10.6%).
  • Canada: 35 of 520 (6.7%) rose to 99 of 545 (18.2%).
Read this with care. The gain belongs to the whole new model. Since 4 November the rules also retry more wordings, for example "LIMIT EXCEEDED" and "REFUSED". How much of the gain comes from the wait and how much from the wider list is NOT ESTABLISHED. The figures were counted twice, from the workbook and from the retry log, on 26 November 2026.

Part 7. Quick answers

A customer was charged again 4 days after a decline. Is that right?

Yes, when the bank said "not now". Retry 1 runs on day 4. A second retry can follow on day 8.

The customer's card expired. Will we keep trying it?

No. An expired or closed card is in the UPDATE group. The customer must add a new card.

The bank said the card was stolen. Will we retry?

No. Lost, stolen and fraud wordings are in the STOP group. We never retry them.

Can we charge on day 20?

No. Nothing is charged after day 14.

Does this apply in every market?

No. Only the United States and Canada.
